Model outputs are statistical estimates from historical data, not guarantees. Every league/market shown here was backtested against real closing odds where available (see reports/soccer_backtest.json and reports/basketball_backtest.json) — beating the market consistently is hard, and this tool is a decision-support / cross-check input, not an autonomous betting system. Live fixtures are pulled from a free third-party schedule feed and matched to our trained team names automatically; if a team name doesn't match cleanly the prediction still runs using a league-average fallback strength for that side. "Why this prediction" panels are built from Elo ratings, the goal model, and real historical results/ head-to-head in our dataset — they do not include injuries, suspensions, or transfer news, since no such feed is integrated. Use edge and Kelly figures as one input among your own trading process, staking limits, and risk controls.
