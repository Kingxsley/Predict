Backtest

Held-out historical performance. Soccer is scored against the de-vigged closing price from real bookmaker data; the AFL is scored against held-out seasons and stated baselines. Lower Brier and log-loss are better. These are the unedited contents of reports/*.json as produced by the training runs.

What the models are

Soccer: a Dixon-Coles bivariate goal model blended with an Elo rating and isotonically calibrated per competition. AFL: an Elo rating plus gradient-boosted margin and total models, with venue familiarity and travel as explicit features. Both trained on historical results only.

What they do not know

No injury, suspension, lineup or roster feed is integrated. Team names from the live schedules are matched to trained names automatically; where a name cannot be matched the prediction still runs on a competition-average strength for that side.

How to use it

These are statistical estimates, not guarantees. Treat edge and Kelly figures as one input into your own process alongside your own staking limits and risk controls, not as an autonomous betting system.
