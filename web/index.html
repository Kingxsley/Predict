Track record

Backtest

Held-out historical performance, scored against the de-vigged closing price from real bookmaker data. Lower Brier and log-loss are better. These are the unedited contents of reports/*.json as produced by the training run. Beating a closing line consistently is hard, and several leagues here do not.

What the model is

Soccer: a Dixon-Coles bivariate goal model blended with an Elo rating and isotonically calibrated per league. NBA: an Elo rating plus a gradient-boosted margin/total model. Trained on historical results only.

What it does not know

No injury, suspension, lineup or transfer feed is integrated. Team names from the live schedule are matched to trained names automatically; where a name cannot be matched the prediction still runs on a league-average strength for that side.

How to use it

These are statistical estimates, not guarantees. Treat edge and Kelly figures as one input into your own process alongside your own staking limits and risk controls, not as an autonomous betting system.
